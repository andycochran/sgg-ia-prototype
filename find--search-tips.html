Search Tips

Keyword search

  • A search bar at the top, with a tip that a minus sign excludes words or phrases (for example, “-research”).
  • Two matching modes: “must include all words” (AND) and “may include any words” (OR).
  • A “Copy this search query” button that puts a shareable link to your current search on your clipboard.

Filters (left sidebar, with result counts)

  • Opportunity status: Forecasted, Open, Closed, or Archived. Forecasted and Open are selected by default.
  • Funding instrument: Cooperative Agreement, Grant, Procurement Contract, or Other.
  • Eligibility: about 17 applicant types, including state, county, and city governments, universities, tribal governments, nonprofits, small businesses, individuals, and “Unrestricted.”
  • Agency: a nested list of departments and sub-agencies, such as HHS (NIH, CDC, FDA, HRSA), Defense, Justice, State, and NSF.
  • Category: topics such as Health, Education, Agriculture, Environment, Energy, Transportation, Housing, and Science and Technology.
  • Posted date: within the last 3, 7, 14, 30, or 60 days.
  • Closing date range: closing in the next 7, 30, 90, or 120 days.
  • Cost sharing: whether the opportunity requires it (Yes or No).
  • A “Clear filters” button resets everything.

Sorting

  • Options include most relevant (the default), close date (soonest or furthest), posted date (newest or oldest), title (A to Z or Z to A), and award minimum or maximum (lowest or highest).
