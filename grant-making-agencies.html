Grant-Making Agencies

Federal funding opportunities are administered by 26 federal grant-making agencies and numerous independent commissions. Each agency oversees programs aligned with its mission, congressional authorizing legislation, and annual budget.

While all federal opportunities are searchable in one place on Simpler Grants.gov, each agency sets its own application guidelines, review criteria, and reporting requirements in its Notice of Funding Opportunity (NOFO).

Looking for opportunities from a specific department? You can filter by agency using our Search tool.

Primary Federal Grant-Making Agencies

AmeriCorps (AC)

Funds national and community service programs that address critical needs in education, public health, disaster relief, and environmental stewardship across local communities.

Department of Agriculture (USDA)

Provides grants and cooperative agreements supporting agriculture, rural development, economic infrastructure, forestry, nutrition programs, and food safety research.

Department of Commerce (DOC)

Drives economic growth, technological innovation, and job creation through agencies like the Economic Development Administration (EDA), NOAA, and NIST.

Department of Defense (DOD)

Awards scientific and technological research grants to academic institutions, nonprofits, and labs to advance national defense, medicine, and engineering capabilities.

Department of Education (ED)

Supports educational equity and achievement by awarding grants to state and local educational agencies, institutions of higher education, and community organizations.

Department of Energy (DOE)

Funds scientific research, clean energy development, grid modernization, and environmental remediation through universities, private industry, and national laboratories.

Department of Health and Human Services (HHS)

The largest grant-making agency in the federal government. HHS supports public health, biomedical research, social services, and child welfare through operating divisions including the NIH, CDC, FDA, SAMHSA, and ACF.

Department of Homeland Security (DHS)

Provides funding through FEMA and other offices to strengthen community preparedness, disaster response, border security, cybersecurity, and emergency medical services.

Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD)

Funds affordable housing programs, community development block grants (CDBG), homelessness prevention initiatives, and fair housing enforcement.

Department of the Interior (DOI)

Administers programs protecting natural and cultural heritage, fish and wildlife conservation, public land management, and tribal governance initiatives.

Department of Justice (DOJ)

Awards funding through the Office of Justice Programs (OJP), COPS, and OVW to enhance public safety, reduce crime, and improve criminal and juvenile justice systems.

Department of Labor (DOL)

Funds workforce training, registered apprenticeships, occupational safety initiatives, and programs serving displaced or underrepresented workers.

Department of State (DOS)

Supports foreign assistance programs, cultural exchange, human rights advocacy, democracy building, and humanitarian relief around the globe.

Department of Transportation (DOT)

Invests in nationwide transportation infrastructure, highway safety, public transit networks, airport expansion, and freight rail modernization.

Department of the Treasury (TREAS)

Administers targeted economic revitalization programs, small business lending initiatives, and funding via the Community Development Financial Institutions (CDFI) Fund.

Department of Veterans Affairs (VA)

Provides grants supporting veterans' healthcare facilities, state veterans homes, adaptive sports, and community initiatives preventing veteran homelessness.

Environmental Protection Agency (EPA)

Awards grants to state and tribal partners, universities, and nonprofits to protect air quality, improve water infrastructure, clean up contaminated land, and support environmental education.

Institute of Museum and Library Services (IMLS)

The primary source of federal support for the nation's museums and libraries, promoting lifelong learning, cultural heritage preservation, and digital access.

National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA)

Funds aeronautics, earth science, space exploration, and STEM education research across colleges, universities, and aerospace research organizations.

National Archives and Records Administration (NARA)

Awards funding through the National Historical Publications and Records Commission (NHPRC) to preserve, publish, and increase access to historical records.

National Endowment for the Arts (NEA)

Dedicated to supporting excellence in the arts, bringing artistic opportunities to communities across the country, and providing leadership in arts education.

National Endowment for the Humanities (NEH)

Supports research, preservation, public programs, and educational initiatives in history, literature, philosophy, and cultural disciplines.

National Science Foundation (NSF)

Supports fundamental research and education across all non-medical fields of science and engineering through awards to academic institutions and researchers.

Small Business Administration (SBA)

Offers grants supporting small business development centers, entrepreneurial training, veteran business outreach, and disaster recovery support.

Social Security Administration (SSA)

Funds research and demonstration projects focusing on retirement, disability policy, employment support for beneficiaries, and economic security.

U.S. Agency for International Development (USAID)

Leads the federal government's international development and humanitarian efforts to reduce poverty, advance democracy, and improve global health.

Independent and Regional Grant-Makers

In addition to the primary cabinet agencies, several independent federal bodies and regional commissions issue grants for targeted geographic or industry-specific initiatives:

  • Appalachian Regional Commission (ARC): Invests in economic development and infrastructure across the 13 Appalachian states.
  • Delta Regional Authority (DRA): Promotes job creation and community development across the Mississippi River Delta region.
  • Inter-American Foundation (IAF): Partners directly with grassroots and community-led initiatives across Latin America and the Caribbean.
  • Northern Border Regional Commission (NBRC): Funds economic and community infrastructure projects in distressed counties of Maine, New Hampshire, New York, and Vermont.
  • U.S. African Development Foundation (USADF): Invests in grassroots agricultural and energy enterprises across Africa.
