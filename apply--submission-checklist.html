Submission Checklist

Before you submit your application, use this checklist to catch the more common mistakes.

Registration

  • Make sure your organization’s SAM.gov registration is active.
  • Confirm that your Unique Entity Identifier (UEI) is active.
  • Register on Grants.gov and link your account to your organization.
  • Designate an Authorized Organization Representative (AOR)

Application Content

  • Review the criteria and point values, and build your narrative around them.
  • Make sure that every required field is completed in forms.
  • Attach all required documents.

Format

  • Follow the required page limits.
  • Use the required font size and margins.
  • Include any optional attachments the NOFO recommends.

Funding Details

  • Check any cost-sharing or matching fund rules.
  • Document your matching funds, if required.
  • Fit your requested amount within the award range.

Final Review

  • Proofread your narrative for clarity and consistency.
  • Confirm your authorized representative will sign and submit the application.
  • Submit before the deadline, with time to spare in case of technical issues.
